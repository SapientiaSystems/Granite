# Taking the Survey

Take Survey asks three questions, each answered on a scale from 1 to 5, and lets you add one suggestion for improvement.

Answer every question, then choose Submit Survey. Your response is recorded with your user name.

Administrators see all responses and the average answer to each question in the Surveys view.
